## 🏢 Executive Summary & Business Problem
In large-scale retail and B2B/B2C e-commerce ecosystems, treating the entire customer base as a homogeneous group leads to inefficient marketing spend and high customer churn. 

This project delivers an automated **Customer Intelligence & Decision Support System (DSS)** that transforms raw transactional ERP/e-commerce logs into actionable customer personas. By combining **RFM (Recency, Frequency, Monetary)** behavioral modeling with unsupervised machine learning (**K-Means Clustering**), the platform empowers executive leadership and CRM teams to:
* **Maximize Customer Lifetime Value (CLV)** through targeted retention strategies.
* **Identify Pareto (80/20) Revenue Drivers** in real time.
* **Prevent VIP Churn** by isolating high-value accounts showing early signs of disengagement.
* **Bridge Data Science & Marketing** via one-click CSV target list exports for CRM campaign automation.

## 🔬 Analytical Methodology & Mathematical Pipeline

### 1. Data Sanitization & Feature Engineering
Raw invoice records undergo strict validation before entering the analytical pipeline:
* **Identity Integrity:** Records missing `CustomerID` values are purged to ensure accurate account-level aggregation.
* **Cancellation & Return Filtering:** Credit notes and cancelled transactions (identified via `'C'` prefixes in `InvoiceNo` and non-positive `Quantity` values) are excluded to prevent distortion of gross spend metrics.
* **Monetary Feature Creation:** Line-item revenue is engineered as `TotalPrice = Quantity * UnitPrice`.

### 2. Customer-Level RFM Aggregation
Transactional logs are grouped by `CustomerID` against a dynamic snapshot reference date (`T_ref = max(InvoiceDate) + 1 day`):
* **Recency (R):** Days elapsed since the customer's last recorded purchase (`T_ref - T_last_invoice`).
* **Frequency (F):** Count of unique valid invoices (`nunique`) generated by the customer.
* **Monetary (M):** Cumulative gross expenditure (`sum(TotalPrice)`) across the analyzed period.

### 3. Skewness Mitigation & Feature Scaling
E-commerce monetary and frequency distributions exhibit heavy positive skewness (power-law distribution). Because K-Means relies on isotropic Euclidean distance calculations, raw features would bias clusters toward extreme outliers:
1. **Logarithmic Transformation:** Applied via `np.log1p` to compress variance and normalize right-skewed distributions (`X_log = ln(X + 1)`).
2. **Z-Score Standardization:** Applied via `StandardScaler` so that Recency (days), Frequency (orders), and Monetary (currency) contribute equally to centroid distance calculations (`Z = (X_log - mean) / std`).

### 4. Unsupervised Clustering (`K-Means`)
The standardized matrix is partitioned into `K = 4` distinct behavioral clusters with a fixed random seed (`random_state=42`) to ensure full deterministic reproducibility across environments.

## 🎯 Customer Segmentation Matrix & CRM Action Playbook

| Segment Persona | Behavioral Profile (RFM) | Business Interpretation | Strategic CRM / Marketing Action |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **🏆 Champions** | **Low R, High F, High M** | Core revenue drivers who purchase frequently and recently at high volume. | Enroll in VIP loyalty programs, offer early access to new releases, avoid unnecessary discount erosion. |
| **💎 At Risk VIPs** | **High R, Med/High F, High M** | Historically high-value accounts ("Whales") showing prolonged inactivity. | Trigger urgent personalized outreach, assign dedicated account managers, offer executive win-back incentives. |
| **🌱 Potential Customers** | **Low R, Low/Med F, Med M** | Recently onboarded or active buyers with moderate basket sizes. | Deploy cross-sell/upsell recommendation engines and basket-threshold discounts to increase purchase frequency. |
| **🚶 Lost Customers** | **High R, Low F, Low M** | Churned one-time or low-engagement buyers. | Relegate to automated, low-cost email workflows; exclude from high-cost paid acquisition budgets. |

## 🖥️ Enterprise Dashboard Architecture (Plotly Dash)

The front-end application (`app.py`) is built with **Plotly Dash** and **Dash Bootstrap Components (`FLATLY` theme)**, structured around three specialized operational views:

### 1. Executive Overview Tab
* **Dynamic KPI Cards:** Real-time calculation of *Active Customers*, *Total Gross Revenue*, *Average Revenue Per User (ARPU)*, and *Average Order Frequency*.
* **Pareto Revenue Contribution (Donut Chart):** Visualizes the exact percentage of total revenue generated by each customer tier.
* **Customer Headcount Distribution (Horizontal Bar):** Compares segment population size against revenue weight.
* **Cross-Segment RFM Benchmarks (Grouped Bar):** Side-by-side comparison of average Recency and Frequency across personas.

### 2. 3D & 2D Cluster Space Tab (Data Science View)
* **3D Spatial Cluster Distribution:** Interactive 3D scatter plot (`Recency` × `Frequency` × `Monetary`) allowing rotational inspection of cluster boundaries and centroid separation.
* **2D Log-Scaled Behavioral Map:** Log-log scatter visualization (`Frequency` vs. `Monetary`) demonstrating clear linear separability of customer tiers.

### 3. CRM Target List & Export Center Tab
* **Interactive Data Grid:** Native sorting, multi-column filtering, and conditional color formatting based on customer criticality.
* **One-Click CSV Export:** Enables marketing and sales operations to filter specific cohorts (e.g., *At Risk VIPs* with Recency < 180 days) and export ready-to-use target lists directly into Excel or CRM tools.

## ⚙️ Installation & Local Deployment

### Prerequisites
* Python 3.10 or higher
* Git

### Step-by-Step Setup

1. **Clone the repository:**
~~~bash
git clone https://github.com/GorkemKutukculer/RFM_KMeans_Segmentation.git
cd RFM_KMeans_Segmentation
~~~

2. **Initialize and activate an isolated virtual environment:**
~~~bash
# Windows
python -m venv venv
venv\Scripts\activate

# macOS / Linux
python3 -m venv venv
source venv/bin/activate
~~~

3. **Install required dependencies:**
~~~bash
pip install -r requirements.txt
~~~

4. **Launch the Enterprise Dash Server:**
~~~bash
python app.py
~~~

5. **Access the Dashboard:**
   Open your browser and navigate to `http://127.0.0.1:8050/`.
